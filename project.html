About Our School

Kemissie Secondary and Preparatory School is located in the heart of Kemissie town, Oromia Zone, Amhara Region. We are dedicated to nurturing young minds and building future leaders.

Our mission is to provide an inclusive, innovative, and safe learning environment where every student can achieve their full potential academically, socially, and emotionally.

Our Vision

"To be a center of academic excellence producing ethical, competent, and responsible citizens."

School History

Established decades ago, Kemissie Secondary School has long been a beacon of knowledge in the region. Starting with just a few classrooms and teachers, it has grown exponentially to accommodate thousands of students from Kemissie and surrounding areas.

Throughout its history, the school has produced notable alumni who have gone on to contribute significantly in various sectors including healthcare, engineering, and leadership.
